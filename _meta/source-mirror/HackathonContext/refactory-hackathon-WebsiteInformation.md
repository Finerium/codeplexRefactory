## About

## Build intense. Learn fast. Ship proud.

A product‑driven hack sprint where engineers, designers & builders jam on meaningful problems. Less theory. More shipping. Mentors keep velocity high and teams focus on real-world impact.

### Key Numbers

- **Hours:** 24+
- **Participants Cap:** 100
- **Focus Tracks:** 1
- **Prize Pool:** 20M IDR

### Challenge: Engineering Productivity x AI

Build real, end-to-end AI-powered solutions that transform how software gets built — from planning and design to development, testing, deployment, and operations. The goal is working products that real users can actually use, not demos or mockups.

**Your mission:** ship a fully functional solution that solves a real software engineering problem with AI. Every feature should work end-to-end — real inputs, real processing, real outputs. Judges will use your product, not just watch a slide deck.

#### What We Expect

- ✓ **Aim for excellence** — build something you'd actually put your name on, not a rushed prototype
- ✓ **Innovate** — bring something that doesn't exist yet, or meaningfully improve on what already does
- ✓ **It works end-to-end** — a real user can get a real outcome from start to finish, using real data and scenarios
- ✓ **It ships as a usable MVP** — judges will run it themselves in real-time, no slides or screenshots, just needs polish after
- ✓ **It's solid** — no breaks on unexpected input, no hand-holding to get it running

### Flow & Momentum

1. **Kickoff & Team Snap** — Prompt reveal, lightning intros, rapid team formation & scope locking.
2. **Sprint & Mentor Loops** — Ship vertical slices early. Mentor desks unblock architecture & UX decisions.
3. **Checkpoint Story Framing** — Refine problem narrative, quantify impact, tighten demo flow.
4. **Final Demos & Pulse** — High‑signal pitches judged on value, execution, originality & clarity.

### What You Get

- **Real-World Experience** — Build and launch a working product in just 24+ Hours.
- **Mentorship & Guidance** — Learn directly from industry experts and professionals.
- **Exposure & Recognition** — Present your project to judges, sponsors, and the wider community.
- **Free Tools & Infrastructure** — Virtual machines and developer frameworks provided.
- **Career & Networking** — Connect with potential employers, collaborators, and future teammates.
- **Prizes & Certificates** — Rewards that recognize your innovation and hard work.

### Evaluation Criteria

- **Innovation & Creativity** — How innovative is the idea itself — fresh approach, unique angle, not just a copy of something that exists
- **Impact & Relevance** — How valuable and impactful is the project, and how relevant it is to the given challenge
- **Technical Execution** — Architecture, code quality, context handling, and whether it's already deployed and running
- **UX & Design** — How the interface looks, how it feels to use, and overall usability for real users
- **Presentation & Communication** — How well the team presents and articulates their idea in front of the judges

## Schedule

## 24+ Hours of Focused Building

A schedule crafted for flow, fast progress, and team alignment — so you can ship your best work.

### Day 1

1. **08:00 – 09:00 — Participant Check-in & Lanyard Distribution**
   Registration • badge pickup • entering the room

2. **09:00 – 10:00 — Official Opening Ceremony & Welcoming Speeches**
   Introductions • event briefing • rules

3. **10:00 – 11:00 — Planning Time**
   Hackathon participants discuss with mentors about their plan for creating the solution they want to design.
   *Output: PRD*

4. **10:00 – 11:00 — Mentor Checking/Discuss**
   Teams meet with a mentor to discuss progress and receive guidance or feedback on their work.

5. **11:00 – 13:00 — Design Time**
   Hackathon participants design the high-level architecture of the solution they want to build.
   *Output: C4 Models*

6. **12:00 — Power Lunch**
   Lunch provided while participants continue networking.

7. **13:00 (Day 1) – 11:00 (Day 2) — Hacking Time**
   Hackathon participants create the specifications for the solution they want to build and convert the specifications into code using an open spec.
   *Output: A functional, deployed app accessible for demo and testing*

8. **18:30 — Snack Drop**
   Evening snack break to keep energy up.

### Day 2

1. **08:00 – 09:00 — Participant Check-in & Return to Venue**
   Re-registration • entering the room.

2. **11:00 — Hacking Time End**
   All hacking stops — time to wrap up.

3. **11:00 – 13:00 — Project End: Submission of All Requirements**
   The team prepares the final project submission by completing the presentation, a demo, and all necessary documentation.

4. **12:00 — Power Lunch**
   Lunch provided while participants continue networking.

5. **13:00 – 15:00 — Meeting to Determine Top 5 Teams**
   Mentors review all submissions and select the top 5 teams for final presentation.

6. **15:00 – 17:00 — Presentation by 5 Teams**
   10 minutes per team • finalists present their projects.

7. **17:00 – 18:30 — Mentor Meeting to Determine Winners**
   Judging round • decide 1st, 2nd, 3rd place.

8. **18:00 — Power Dinner**
   Dinner provided while participants continue networking.

9. **19:00 – 19:30 — Announcing the Winners & Documentation**
   Awarding • photo session.

10. **20:00 — Closing of the Event**
    Official closing remarks.

### The Hackathon Flow

A hackathon isn't just nonstop coding — it's a rhythm of building, sharing, and refining. With clear phases and mentor touchpoints, teams stay focused, avoid burnout, and deliver their best ideas on stage.

Phases:
- Kickoff Spark
- First Launch
- Checkpoints
- Mentor Boosts
- Final Prep
- Grand Demo

## FAQ

## Common Questions

Everything else — just ask us on Instagram.

**Q1: Do I need to pay to participate?**
No. Refactory Hackathon is free of charge.

**Q2: Is individual participation allowed?**
No. Each participant must be part of a team of 2–4 members.

**Q3: Do I need to come from a technology or IT background?**
No. We welcome participants from all educational backgrounds.

**Q4: Can I use code or projects I have already created?**
No. All projects must be developed during the hackathon, except for open-source libraries.

**Q5: What if one of my team members cannot attend on the last day?**
The entire team must attend the final presentation. Absence will be considered as withdrawal.

**Q6: Will participation certificates be provided?**
To be announced by the committee.

**Q7: Will food and drinks be provided?**
To be announced by the committee. Please bring personal supplies as backup.
