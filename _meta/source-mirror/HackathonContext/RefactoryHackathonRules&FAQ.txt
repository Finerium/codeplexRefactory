Team Naming Guidelines:
1. Team names must be respectful and appropriate for a professional event.
2. Avoid names containing offensive, discriminatory, or sensitive terms related to gender, ethnicity, religion, race, or sexual orientation.
3. No vulgar, explicit, or provocative wording.
4. Names should not reference hate speech, harassment, or stereotypes.
5. The organizing committee reserves the right to request a name change if a team name is considered inappropriate.

General Terms & Conditions
1. Eligibility
* Refactory Hackathon is open to everyone aged up to 28 years.
* Eligible participants are high school and university students in their final year or semester, as well as fresh graduates, gap-year individuals, and non-traditional learners.
2. Team Size
* Each team must consist of 2–4 members.
* Individual participation is not allowed.
3. Team Name Guidelines
* Team names must not contain:
* Content related to ethnicity, religion, race, or intergroup sensitivity (SARA).
* Provocative or hateful speech.
* Violations of social norms.
4. Team Representation
* There is no limit on the number of teams representing the same institution.
5. One Team per Participant
* Each participant may only be registered in one team.
6. Expenses
* All transportation and accommodation expenses are the responsibility of participants.
7. Final Presentation Attendance
* Teams that do not attend the final presentation session will be considered withdrawn.
8. Cheating and Plagiarism
The following are strictly prohibited:
* Plagiarism or use of non-original code.
* Use of pre-existing non-open-source code.
* Receiving help from outside the team.
* Outsourcing projects to external parties.
9. Policy Changes
* Rules may be revised at any time by the committee as needed.
10. Final Decisions
* All decisions made by the committee and judges are final and binding.

📘 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
* Q: Is it mandatory for all participants to attend onsite?
* A: Yes, all participants are required to be physically present at the venue during the event.
* Q: Do I need to pay to participate?
* A: No. Refactory Hackathon is free of charge.
* Q: Is individual participation allowed?
* A: No. Each participant must be part of a team of 2–4 members.
* Q: Do I need to come from a technology or IT background?
* A: No. We welcome participants from all educational backgrounds.
* Q: Can I use code or projects I have already created?
* A: No. All projects must be developed during the hackathon, except for open-source libraries.
* Q: What if one of my team members cannot attend on the last day?
* A: The entire team must attend the final presentation. Absence will be considered as withdrawal.
* Q: Will participation certificates be provided?
* A: To be announced by the committee.
* Q: Will food and drinks be provided?
* A: To be announced by the committee. Please bring personal supplies as backup.
* Q: At Refactory Hackathon, do we have to use all the tech stacks listed in the requirements?
* A: Yes, all are mandatory. Except for mobile—choose one (Android or iOS).
