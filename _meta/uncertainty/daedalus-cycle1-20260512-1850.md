## C1: Fog vs HDRI race condition (medium concern)

**Confidence**: 70 percent on chosen pattern.

**Concern**: r3f issue threads document a race where the JSX `<fog>` primitive can fail to attach when an Environment preset Suspense boundary takes more than one frame to resolve. The symptom: fog briefly does not apply, the scene flashes flat dark, then snaps to fog. Visually jarring on the first 5-second cinematic intro per PRD stretch tier 1.

**Mitigation chosen**: imperative `scene.fog = new Fog(...)` inside useEffect, cleanup unsets. This guarantees fog applies after the scene is mounted plus after HDRI Suspense resolves (the useEffect fires after the first commit).

**Residual risk**: if Iris BuildingInstances unmounts the scene via Suspense fallback (unlikely; their data is mocked Wave 1, not async), the fog cleanup fires and the scene briefly renders without fog. Iris should not throw Suspense; if they do, Daedalus V2 wraps fog in a never-cleanup ref instead. Eunomia can flag if observed.

**Validation**: pnpm dev, refresh `/__daedalus_smoke` 5 times, watch for fog flash. Eunomia adds to audit checklist.

## C2: PerformanceMonitor `factor` semantics under sudden GPU pressure (medium concern)

**Confidence**: 75 percent on threshold tuning.

**Concern**: Drei PerformanceMonitor delivers a factor that increases or decreases by `step` (default 0.1) each iteration where the average FPS crosses the bounds. Daedalus tuned thresholds to 0.85 / 0.70 / 0.55. On a sudden GPU spike (e.g., the Sparkles tier-3 first frame), factor may dip below 0.70 within 250 ms x 10 iterations = 2.5 seconds, dropping pixel ratio rapidly. This is fine semantically (faster recovery) but the visual flash may look like a glitch during pitch demo.

**Mitigation chosen**: 0.1 hysteresis band on each threshold to prevent flapping. Hysteresis means once dropped, factor must climb 0.1 higher than the drop threshold before restoring.

**Residual risk**: if the laptop is throttling thermally (M-series Pro 16GB heating up after 2 hours of dev), factor may stay below 0.85 permanently. Demo workflow: open the lid 10 minutes pre-demo to let thermals settle. Hafiz already knows.

**Validation**: Eunomia H1 audit benchmarks the steady-state factor with 300 buildings. Daedalus does not pre-tune below the suggested values; Pan post-Wave 3 may revisit if demo lag observed during rehearsal.

## C3: AdaptiveDpr plus PerformanceMonitor double-write to `setDpr` (medium concern)

**Confidence**: 75 percent on coexistence safety.

**Concern**: Drei `<AdaptiveDpr pixelated />` reduces device pixel ratio when r3f's `state.performance.current` is below max. Daedalus also writes pixel ratio via `setDpr([1, 2])` or `setDpr(1)` from the PerformanceMonitor onChange ladder. Both writers target the same r3f setDpr. If both fire in opposite directions in adjacent frames, the dpr may oscillate.

**Mitigation chosen**: keep both wired. AdaptiveDpr handles instantaneous regress events (camera drag); PerformanceMonitor handles slower factor-driven drift. They write at different cadences (AdaptiveDpr per frame during regress, PerformanceMonitor per onChange iteration which is debounced to ms 250 default).

**Residual risk**: if the audit shows visible dpr oscillation, drop AdaptiveDpr (single line removal) and rely solely on PerformanceMonitor ladder. Daedalus does not pre-emptively drop it because AdaptiveDpr also covers the case where user drags during a moment the factor is above 0.7 threshold (PerformanceMonitor would not have dropped dpr but the drag still benefits from temporary reduction).

**Validation**: Eunomia adds "drag camera vigorously for 5 seconds, watch dpr in r3f-perf overlay for oscillation" to audit checklist.
